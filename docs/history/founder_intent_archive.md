# Founder Intent Archive (Immutable, Non-Authoritative)

> This archive is context, not law.
> It may not be cited as authority.
> It exists to prevent myth-making and bad-faith reinterpretation.

## Purpose

Preserve intent, trade-offs, and uncertainty at the moment of creation.
Future contributors may disagree with it.
They may not pretend it never existed.

## Founder Statement

TrailMthr exists to serve people who move through the world physically and intentionally.
It is built for those who value autonomy, truth in data, and mutual respect over convenience, scale, or profit.

I reject systems that:
- require trust in rulers
- depend on centralized permission
- trade user sovereignty for growth
- rewrite history to justify power

TrailMthr must remain forkable, inspectable, and survivable without me.

If TrailMthr ever requires my approval, memory, or endorsement to function correctly, it has failed.

## Non-Negotiable Principles (Founder View)

- No hidden authority
- No silent enforcement
- No compulsory identity
- No irreversible decisions
- No punishment systems

## Accepted Trade-Offs

- Slower growth over compromised integrity
- Fragmentation over capture
- Redundancy over efficiency
- Transparency over polish

## Known Risks at Founding

- Governance fatigue
- Low participation density
- Hostile forks masquerading as legitimacy
- Economic pressure to centralize

These risks were accepted knowingly.

## Rejected Alternatives (At Founding)

- Permanent admin/moderator roles
- Emergency powers for “critical moments”
- Account linking to social platforms as identity proof
- Centralized “official” datasets that cannot be forked
