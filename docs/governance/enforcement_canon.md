# TrailMthr Enforcement Canon

**Status:** Canonical Governance Artifact
**Applies To:** All TrailMthr enforcement logic, present and future
**Precedence:** Supersedes product requirements, implementation details, and operational policy where conflicts arise
**Lexical Authority:** Governed by *GOVERNANCE_LEXICON.md*

---

## 1. Purpose

This Enforcement Canon defines **when**, **how**, and **to what extent** TrailMthr may refuse, limit, or withdraw its own services. It establishes hard constraints that prevent coercion, prediction, punishment, or moral judgment.

TrailMthr enforcement is **non-coercive by design** and exists solely to preserve the integrity of voluntary contracts.

---

## 2. Non‑Goals (Explicit Prohibitions)

TrailMthr enforcement **will never**:

* Predict future behavior or risk
* Infer intent or character
* Determine crimes or wrongdoing
* Monitor users outside explicit consent
* Impose punishment or sanctions
* Coordinate with law‑enforcement modes
* Create reputation or trust scores
* Enforce morality, norms, or laws

These are permanent non‑goals.

---

## 3. Foundational Principles (Immutable)

1. **Contract Supremacy** — Enforcement may only occur pursuant to a signed, active contract.
2. **Consent Primacy** — Verification requires explicit, purpose‑bound consent.
3. **Refusal Only** — Enforcement actions are limited to refusal or withdrawal of TrailMthr services.
4. **Scope Limitation** — Enforcement must be feature‑scoped and proportional.
5. **User Benefit Under Uncertainty** — Ambiguity resolves in favor of the user.
6. **Exit Without Penalty** — Users may leave at any time with data retrieval.
7. **Emergency Inviolability** — Emergency functions are never restricted.

These principles may not be altered without constitutional amendment.

---

## 4. Enforcement Preconditions

All enforcement actions **must** satisfy *every* condition below:

* A **Contract** exists and is **Active**
* The contract explicitly defines:

  * Permitted **Verification** methods
  * Enforcement **Scope**
* Required **Evidence** is available through consented channels
* The specific **Claim** under evaluation cannot be verified

If any condition is unmet, enforcement is prohibited.

---

## 5. Permitted Enforcement Actions

The following actions are allowed:

* Refusal to attest to a claim
* Refusal to provide enhanced functionality
* Withdrawal of trust scoped to a contract or feature
* Display of transparent refusal explanations

The following actions are **forbidden**:

* Account‑wide punishment
* Cross‑domain restrictions
* Hidden throttling or shadow bans
* Feature removal unrelated to the contract

---

## 6. Enforcement Scope Rules

* Enforcement must be **feature‑scoped** to the violated contract
* Core Functionality may not be restricted
* Enhanced Functionality may be refused
* Emergency Functions may never be restricted

No enforcement action may expand beyond its declared scope.

---

## 7. Verification Rules

* Verification may only evaluate **Claims**, not users
* Verification is discrete, not continuous, unless explicitly contracted
* Verification ceases immediately upon **Revoked Consent**
* Historical data may not be reinterpreted under new rules

Inference and prediction are prohibited.

---

## 8. Refusal Transparency

Every refusal must include:

* The specific contract ID
* The unmet condition
* The affected feature(s)
* A remediation or exit path, if applicable

Silent refusals are forbidden.

---

## 9. Human Roles and Limits

### Admins

* Maintain systems only
* May not enforce, revoke, or restrict access

### Moderators

* May review disputes
* May only restore access
* May never impose enforcement

### Overrides

* Overrides may pause or reverse refusal
* Overrides may only benefit the user
* All overrides are logged and auditable

---

## 10. Failure Handling

* System uncertainty defaults to **no enforcement**
* Technical errors may not be framed as violations
* Data unavailability invalidates enforcement

---

## 11. External Pressure Resistance

TrailMthr does not:

* Provide enforcement APIs to third parties
* Accept external enforcement requests
* Certify legal compliance

Only voluntary contracts are enforceable.

---

## 12. Language and Framing

All enforcement communication must use **Neutral System Voice** as defined in the Governance Lexicon.

Forbidden framing includes moral, criminal, or behavioral language.

---

## 13. Auditability

All enforcement logic must be:

* Deterministic
* Reproducible
* Auditable against contracts and evidence

Opaque models are forbidden in enforcement paths.

---

## 14. Amendment Rule

This canon may be amended only through the constitutional process defined in *TRAILMTHR_CONSTITUTION.md*.

---

## Closing Statement

> **TrailMthr enforces agreements, not people.**
> **Its power is the ability to refuse participation — nothing more.**

---

*End of Enforcement Canon*
