# TrailMthr Governance Lexicon

**Status:** Canonical Governance Artifact
**Applies To:** All TrailMthr systems, code, documentation, policy, UI copy, and governance materials
**Precedence:** This lexicon supersedes informal, colloquial, legal, or industry-standard interpretations of the same terms

---

## Purpose

This lexicon establishes a **controlled vocabulary** for TrailMthr governance. Its purpose is to:

* Prevent semantic drift over time
* Eliminate accidental or intentional misinterpretation
* Bind technical implementation to ethical intent
* Remove ambiguity as a vector for power expansion

All enforcement, consent, and governance decisions **must** use these definitions verbatim. Substituting near-synonyms is prohibited unless explicitly allowed.

---

## Core Principle

> **Words define power.**
> Therefore, words used in TrailMthr governance are treated as binding constraints, not stylistic choices.

---

## Lexical Reference Standard

### Reference Dictionary

All base word meanings in this lexicon are grounded in the following reference works, **as of the publication date of this document**:

* **Merriam-Webster Unabridged Dictionary** (primary)
* **Oxford English Dictionary (OED)** (historical and etymological reference)

Where TrailMthr definitions diverge from common usage, **the TrailMthr definition explicitly overrides** external dictionaries.

**Rationale:**
Language drifts over time. By anchoring this lexicon to specific reference works at a fixed point, TrailMthr prevents retroactive reinterpretation of intent.

---

## Canonical Terms

### 1. Enforcement

**Definition:**
A system action in which TrailMthr refuses, limits, or withdraws its own services pursuant to a signed and active contract.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Punishment
* Discipline
* Moral judgment
* Legal adjudication

**Why This Term Exists:**
To distinguish system self-limitation from coercive control.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Penalty, sanction, consequence, punishment, corrective action

---

### 2. Consent

**Definition:**
An explicit, informed, revocable agreement by a user allowing TrailMthr to perform a narrowly defined action for a stated purpose.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Implied agreement
* Bundled acceptance
* Continued usage assumption

**Why This Term Exists:**
To ensure all data use and verification is voluntary and purpose-bound.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Opt-in (when vague), acceptance, agreement (without scope)

---

### 3. Contract

**Definition:**
A versioned, explicit agreement defining obligations, permissions, verification methods, and enforcement scope between a user and TrailMthr.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Terms of service (unless explicitly designated)
* Implied rules
* Platform norms

**Why This Term Exists:**
To bind enforcement exclusively to voluntary agreements.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Policy, rule, guideline

---

### 4. Verification

**Definition:**
A contract-authorized process by which TrailMthr checks whether a specific claim can be substantiated using consented data sources.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Surveillance
* Monitoring
* Continuous observation

**Why This Term Exists:**
To limit data evaluation to claim-specific validation.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Tracking, monitoring, inspection

---

### 5. Claim

**Definition:**
A discrete assertion made by a user or system that requires validation before being relied upon.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Belief
* Intent
* Prediction

**Why This Term Exists:**
To focus system logic on statements, not people.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Behavior, profile

---

### 6. Refusal

**Definition:**
A transparent system decision to not provide a specific service or attestation due to unmet contract conditions.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Punishment
* Ban
* Suspension

**Why This Term Exists:**
To frame enforcement as non-coercive withdrawal.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Block, lockout, penalty

---

### 7. Trust

**Definition:**
A conditional state in which TrailMthr is willing to rely on and extend services based on validated claims.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Moral approval
* Reputation score

**Why This Term Exists:**
To express reliance without judgment.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Credibility rating, score

---

### 8. Trust Withdrawal

**Definition:**
The removal of TrailMthr’s reliance on specific claims or accounts due to unverifiable contract conditions.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Punishment
* Ostracism
* Blacklisting

**Why This Term Exists:**
To allow disengagement without condemnation.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Blacklist, strike

---

### 9. User

**Definition:**
A human individual who voluntarily interacts with TrailMthr systems.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Subject
* Actor
* Entity

**Why This Term Exists:**
To center human agency.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Subject, account-holder (when dehumanizing)

---

### 10. Account

**Definition:**
A technical construct representing a user’s interactions, contracts, and data within TrailMthr.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* The user themselves
* A legal identity

**Why This Term Exists:**
To separate system objects from people.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Person, individual

---

### 11. Evidence

**Definition:**
Data explicitly provided by the user or obtained through consented verification channels.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Inferred signals
* Correlated behavior
* External suspicion

**Why This Term Exists:**
To constrain what the system may rely upon.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Signal, indicator

---

### 12. Inference

**Definition:**
Any conclusion drawn without explicit user-provided or consented evidence.

**Status:**
**Prohibited in enforcement logic.**

---

### 13. Prediction

**Definition:**
Any estimation of future behavior, risk, or intent.

**Status:**
**Explicitly forbidden in governance and enforcement.**

---

### 14. Admin

**Definition:**
A technical role with system maintenance capabilities.

**Limitations:**
Admins may not enforce contracts or revoke access.

---

### 15. Moderator

**Definition:**
A role permitted to review disputes and restore access only.

**Limitations:**
Moderators may never impose enforcement.

---

### 16. Override

**Definition:**
A human action that reverses or pauses automated refusal.

**Constraint:**
Overrides may only benefit the user.

---

### 17. Exit

**Definition:**
The ability for a user to leave TrailMthr systems and retrieve their data without penalty.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Loss of functionality as leverage
* Delayed access
* Conditional release

**Why This Term Exists:**
To ensure participation remains voluntary at all times.

**Status:**
Always guaranteed.

---

### 18. Core Functionality

**Definition:**
Essential TrailMthr capabilities required for basic operation, safety, and offline use.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Trust-based features
* Community attestations
* Enhanced analytics

**Why This Term Exists:**
To prevent coercion via deprivation of essential tools.

**Forbidden Substitutions:**
Baseline access, minimum service

---

### 19. Enhanced Functionality

**Definition:**
Optional features dependent on active trust, contracts, or community participation.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Required access
* Core safety features

**Why This Term Exists:**
To scope enforcement impact narrowly.

---

### 20. Emergency Function

**Definition:**
Any feature designed to assist during imminent risk to life or health.

**Constraint:**
Emergency functions may never be restricted by enforcement.

---

### 21. Lifecycle State

**Definition:**
A discrete, explicit phase in the existence of a contract, activity, or system process.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Implicit status
* Assumed continuity

**Why This Term Exists:**
To prevent ambiguous enforcement timing.

---

### 22. Active Contract

**Definition:**
A contract currently in force, unexpired, and unrevoked.

---

### 23. Expired Contract

**Definition:**
A contract that has reached its defined end and confers no enforcement authority.

---

### 24. Revoked Consent

**Definition:**
The explicit withdrawal of previously granted consent.

**Constraint:**
Revocation immediately halts related verification.

---

### 25. Warning

**Definition:**
A non-binding informational notice describing risk or uncertainty.

**Does NOT Mean:**

* Directive
* Requirement

---

### 26. Advisory

**Definition:**
Optional guidance offered without enforcement consequences.

---

### 27. User Benefit Under Uncertainty

**Definition:**
A principle requiring that ambiguous conditions resolve in favor of the user.

---

### 28. Capability Ceiling

**Definition:**
An explicit upper bound on what TrailMthr systems are permitted to do, regardless of technical feasibility.

---

### 29. Forbidden Framing

**Definition:**
Language that moralizes, criminalizes, or anthropomorphizes system decisions.

**Examples:**
Bad actor, offender, violation severity

---

### 30. Neutral System Voice

**Definition:**
Language describing system actions factually without judgment.

---

## Prohibited Terminology

The following terms may not be used in governance, enforcement logic, UI, or documentation:

* Punishment
* Sanction
* Offender
* Crime prediction
* Risk score
* Trust score
* Compliance
* Surveillance
* Policing
