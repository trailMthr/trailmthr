# Non-Punitive Enforcement Doctrine

## Principle

TrailMthr does not punish behavior.
TrailMthr refuses to amplify behavior that violates its principles.

Enforcement is constraint + consequence, not coercion.

## What Enforcement Is

Enforcement is:
- mechanical
- observable
- reversible
- non-discretionary (rule-based)

Enforcement actions include:
- refusing to index content
- refusing to weight content
- refusing to propagate content
- decaying influence signals over time
- expiring capabilities

## What Enforcement Is Not

Enforcement is not:
- banning
- shaming
- compelled deletion
- secret blacklists
- manual moderation without audit trails
- “emergency powers”

## Refusal Rules

When the system refuses something, it should:
- avoid creating drama incentives (no “you are banned” state)
- expose deterministic reasons in an inspectable way
- allow appeal only via evidence, not persuasion
- allow coexistence of forks rather than forced conformity

## Transparency Requirement

All enforcement must be:
- representable as data (events)
- queryable
- attributable to a rule version
- auditable after the fact

## Reversibility Requirement

All enforcement effects must be reversible unless explicitly locked under the Constitutional Change Process.

## Forbidden Mechanisms

- Silent moderation
- Non-audited overrides
- Privileged users with enforcement bypass
- “Trust me” pathways
