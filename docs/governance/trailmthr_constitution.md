# TrailMthr Constitution

**Status:** Foundational Governance Instrument
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** Upon signature

---

## Preamble

TrailMthr is a voluntary system designed to support human autonomy, trust, and coordination without coercion. This Constitution exists to bind the TrailMthr system — present and future — to its founding intent, regardless of scale, ownership, leadership, or technological change.

This document does not seek permission from law, culture, or convenience. It establishes internal limits on power as a matter of design.

---

## Article I — Scope and Authority

1. This Constitution governs all TrailMthr systems, software, services, documentation, and agents.
2. It supersedes all conflicting policies, product requirements, operational practices, and future designs.
3. No component of TrailMthr may claim exemption from this Constitution.

---

## Article II — Foundational Principles (Immutable)

The following principles are permanent and may not be altered except through the constitutional amendment process defined herein:

1. **Voluntary Participation** — No user is compelled to participate in TrailMthr.
2. **Consent-First Design** — All data use and verification require explicit consent.
3. **Non-Coercive Enforcement** — TrailMthr enforces agreements, not people.
4. **Refusal-Only Power** — TrailMthr’s maximum power is to refuse its own participation.
5. **User Benefit Under Uncertainty** — Ambiguity resolves in favor of the user.
6. **Exit Without Penalty** — Users may leave at any time with their data.
7. **Emergency Inviolability** — Emergency functions are never restricted.
8. **Human-Centered Design** — System objects may never be conflated with people.

---

## Article III — Lexical Authority

1. All governance, enforcement, and system communication shall use terms as defined in **GOVERNANCE_LEXICON.md**.
2. Where common, legal, or industry usage conflicts with the lexicon, the lexicon prevails.
3. Semantic drift shall not be grounds for reinterpretation of intent.

---

## Article IV — Enforcement Authority

1. Enforcement authority is strictly limited to the rules defined in **ENFORCEMENT_CANON.md**.
2. No enforcement may occur without a signed, active contract.
3. Punishment, prediction, inference, and moral judgment are prohibited.
4. Enforcement actions are limited to refusal or withdrawal of TrailMthr services.

---

## Article V — Acknowledged Failure Modes

1. Known risks and abuse patterns are enumerated in **FAILURE_MODES_APPENDIX.md**.
2. Denial of a documented failure mode is prohibited as justification for expansion of power.
3. All future designs must demonstrate compatibility with known failure modes.

---

## Article VI — Non-Goals (Permanent)

TrailMthr shall never attempt to:

* Predict crimes or wrongdoing
* Score or rank humans
* Monitor users without consent
* Enforce laws or moral norms
* Act as law enforcement or regulator
* Restrict movement or bodily autonomy
* Withhold emergency assistance

These non-goals are binding.

---

## Article VII — Amendment Process

1. Amendments must be explicit, written, and versioned.
2. Proposed amendments must include:

   * Rationale
   * Impact analysis
   * Compatibility assessment with existing principles
3. Amendments affecting Foundational Principles require:

   * Extended review period
   * Public notice
   * Explicit acknowledgment of tradeoffs
4. Emergency amendments are prohibited.

---

## Article VIII — Continuity Beyond the Founder

1. This Constitution is designed to remain binding regardless of founder involvement.
2. Transfer of ownership, governance, or control does not invalidate this document.
3. Any successor must explicitly accept this Constitution to operate TrailMthr systems.

---

## Article IX — Interpretation

1. Interpretation shall favor restraint over expansion of power.
2. In conflicts between capability and principle, principle prevails.
3. Silence shall not be interpreted as permission.

---

## Closing Declaration

TrailMthr exists to enable trust without domination. This Constitution binds the system to that purpose, even when doing so is inconvenient or costly.

---

*End of TrailMthr Constitution*
