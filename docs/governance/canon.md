# TrailMthr Governance Canon

> This canon is not a grant of authority.
> It is a set of constraints and definitions designed to prevent capture, corruption, and myth-making.
>
> Nothing here creates rulers.
> Everything here removes excuses.

## Core Doctrine

TrailMthr is not governed by people.
TrailMthr is governed by constraints.

The system survives its founder by:
- refusing hidden authority
- refusing silent enforcement
- preserving forkability
- preserving immutable historical record
- defining change as slow, auditable, and reversible

## Scope

This canon defines:
- what enforcement means (and what it must never become)
- how power is expressed without roles
- how change is allowed without capture
- how the system defends itself against centralization pressure

This canon does **not** define:
- UI behavior
- community culture
- marketing strategy
- individual moral codes

Those may evolve without altering the constraints here.

## Non-Negotiable Principles

- No hidden authority
- No silent enforcement
- No compulsory identity
- No irreversible decisions (without an explicit, audited, and rare “lock” mechanism)
- No punishment systems

## Definitions

- **Refusal:** the system’s non-punitive response where it does not amplify, weight, propagate, or prioritize an action/data.
- **Amplification:** making something more visible, more trusted, more discoverable, or more influential than it would be by default.
- **Capability:** a narrow, revocable, time/usage-bounded allowance to perform a single-purpose action.
- **Constitutional change:** any change that alters governance constraints, enforcement doctrine, capability model, or change process.
- **Forkability:** the persistent ability for users/devs to exit with their data and continue independently without loss.
